How to read the records

Start with the legal entity. Operators can trade under several brands. Each profile identifies the entity named in the source and links to the regulator’s record.

Check the scope and date. A licence applies to the jurisdiction and activity the issuing authority specifies. An enforcement publication can describe a proposal, settlement or final decision. Read its status and source before drawing a conclusion.

Read counts in context. Regulators publish different records over different periods. A blank field means the edition lacks that information. An operator’s absence does not establish its compliance.
